# NexaConnect Internet Plans — Q&A

## Available Plans

### Q: What internet plans does NexaConnect offer?
A: NexaConnect offers three plans:

- **Home 300 (NC-300):** Up to 300 Mbps download and 30 Mbps upload for $40 per month.
- **Home 500 (NC-500):** Up to 500 Mbps download and 50 Mbps upload for $55 per month.
- **Home Gigabit (NC-1000):** Up to 1,000 Mbps download and 100 Mbps upload for $75 per month.

Availability and current offers depend on the customer's service address.

### Q: Which plan is suitable for basic internet use?
A: Home 300 may suit everyday browsing, email, HD streaming, and ordinary video calls.

### Q: Which plan is suitable for a household with several devices?
A: Home 500 may suit households with several connected devices, frequent streaming, remote work, and video conferencing.

### Q: Which plan is suitable for heavy internet use?
A: Home Gigabit may suit households with many high-bandwidth devices, heavy simultaneous usage, and large downloads.

### Q: Which plan is best for working from home?
A: Home 300 may be enough for basic browsing and video calls. Home 500 may be better when several people or devices use the connection or video conferencing is frequent.

### Q: Are the advertised speeds guaranteed?
A: No. The listed speeds are maximum advertised speeds. Actual performance may vary.

## Prices and Fees

### Q: How much does Home 300 cost per month?
A: The plan costs $40 per month. Optional router rental brings the subtotal to $48 per month before taxes. Technician installation is listed at $49.

### Q: How much does Home 500 cost per month?
A: The plan costs $55 per month. Optional router rental brings the subtotal to $63 per month before taxes. Technician installation is listed at $49.

### Q: How much does Home Gigabit cost per month?
A: The plan costs $75 per month. Optional router rental brings the subtotal to $83 per month before taxes. Installation is listed at $0, subject to eligibility.

### Q: Is router rental included in the monthly plan price?
A: No. Router rental is optional and costs $8 per month.

### Q: Can I use my own router?
A: Yes, customers may use their own compatible router. Compatibility and the current equipment policy should be confirmed before ordering.

### Q: Is installation free?
A: Home 300 and Home 500 list a $49 technician installation fee. Home Gigabit lists a $0 installation fee, subject to eligibility. The fee should be confirmed for the customer's address and current offer.

### Q: Are taxes included in the prices?
A: No. Listed monthly prices exclude taxes and any charges not specified here.

### Q: Are there any other fees or discounts?
A: This document does not specify all possible fees, promotions, or discounts. Final pricing and current offers should be confirmed before enrollment.

## Data and Contract

### Q: Do the plans have data limits?
A: The listed plans state unlimited data. The current data policy should be confirmed before enrollment.

### Q: Is there a contract?

A: This document does not specify early termination fees. Check the current terms for the selected plan.

## Comparing and Choosing Plans

### Q: What is the difference between Home 300 and Home 500?
A: Home 300 offers up to 300 Mbps download and 30 Mbps upload for $40 per month. Home 500 offers up to 500 Mbps download and 50 Mbps upload for $55 per month. Both list a $49 technician installation fee and optional router rental for $8 per month.

### Q: What is the difference between Home 500 and Home Gigabit?
A: Home 500 offers up to 500 Mbps download and 50 Mbps upload for $55 per month. Home Gigabit offers up to 1,000 Mbps download and 100 Mbps upload for $75 per month. Home 500 lists a $49 installation fee, while Home Gigabit lists $0 installation subject to eligibility. Both offer optional router rental for $8 per month.

### Q: Can I change my plan before placing the order?
A: The available options can be reviewed and the selected plan can be changed before consent and order submission.

### Q: What should I check before agreeing to a plan?
A: Review the plan name, speeds, monthly price, router costs, installation fee, taxes, other charges, contract terms, data policy, promotions, and address-specific conditions. Confirm any details that are not available rather than assuming them.
